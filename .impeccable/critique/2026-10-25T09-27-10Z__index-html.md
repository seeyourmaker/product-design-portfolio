## Overall Impression

The work is specific. The frame around it is a dark, gradient, purple-pill portfolio template, and the sentences in that frame could sit on any designer's site. A hiring manager meets "A PRODUCT DESIGNER CRAFTING INTUITIVE EXPERIENCES AT THE INTERSECTION OF UX & AI." before they meet a single project. The single biggest opportunity is to let the case-study voice and the project artifacts occupy the first screen, and to stop giving "01" and "FEATURED PROJECTS" more type than the work itself.

## What's Working

1. **The case-study headlines are already the portfolio.** "I wanted a Jira chatbot. So I built one." and "Teaching an AI assistant when to act—and when to ask." are concrete, and the numbered sections read like a designer thinking.
2. **The JiraBro and ADAPT thumbnails are drawn, not stocked.** They use project-specific linework and accents. That is the closest the homepage gets to Experience mode.
3. **Evidence is labelled, and the interaction scaffolding is real.** Quick Look, native dialog, skip links, and reduced-motion guards exist. The case studies distinguish product UI from illustrative diagrams.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P0] The two jobs of the site end in placeholders

**What.** About renders `<ContactButton>`, which is "Get in touch" linking to `#contact`. Nav "Contact" uses the same href. The footer is "Have something in mind?", "Let's create.", and "Contact details coming soon." Catalog Curator is a featured card whose thumbnail label is "Ease up the experience!" and whose case-study route is not a case study, so the visitor lands back on the homepage.

**Why it matters.** A portfolio has to show the work and make contact possible. Both paths currently stop.

**Fix.** Put a real email or LinkedIn in `#contact`. Remove Catalog Curator from `featuredProjects` in `src/data/projects.ts` until it has a real study, or mark it in progress and remove the case-study control. Give unknown `/projects/*` paths an explicit missing state.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable harden`

### 2. [P1] The homepage is a generic shell, and the work starts too late

**What.** Render order in `App` is Hero, Marquee, About, Services, then `#projects`. The first thing after the name is other people's MotionSites templates. About floats glass 3D icons. The pill, the grey gradient wordmark, the centered uppercase sections, and the identical rounded cards are the current AI-portfolio kit. Purple `#cf75ff` and `#7621b0` are the accent for all of it.

**Why it matters.** In an Experience portfolio the artifact should lead. Here the first impression is a template, and one click on the marquee leaves the site.

**Fix.** Remove `Marquee`, `marqueeItems`, and the `.decor` images. Place Featured Projects directly after a short hero. Keep the avatar. Collapse About and Expertise to a short block after the work. Let each project's own accent show on the homepage the way it already does inside the case study.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable distill`, then `/impeccable layout`

### 3. [P1] Hierarchy is inverted, and there is only one display size

**What.** From `src/style.css`:

- `h1, h2`: weight 900, uppercase, line-height 1, tracking −0.04em. `h2` is `clamp(3rem, 12vw, 160px)` and centered. "FEATURED PROJECTS" can be 160px.
- `.number`: `clamp(3rem, 10vw, 140px)`, weight 900. On the live card, "01" dwarfs the title.
- `.project-header h3`: `clamp(1rem, 2.3vw, 2.5rem)`, weight 500, uppercase. The project name maxes out near 40px.
- `.hero-subtitle`: "A Product Designer", weight 500, up to 2rem.
- `.hero-bottom p`: the same idea again, weight 300, uppercase, tracking 0.025em, `clamp(0.75rem, 1.4vw, 1.5rem)`. On a 639px screen that intro drops to `clamp(0.625rem, 2.5vw, 0.75rem)`, about 10–12px.
- `.service p`: weight 300, opacity 0.6. `.project-description`: weight 300, opacity 0.72. `.category`: 14px, weight 300, opacity 0.65.
- Card actions at `max-width: 767px`: `.live-button` is 10px.

The case studies use a different scale: mixed case, weights 500–700, a real italic on "So I built one." The homepage never picks up that scale, so the site feels like two type systems.

**Why it matters.** The eye hits the section label and the index, then has to hunt for the project. Dimmed 300-weight body copy makes the sentences that should persuade look secondary. On a phone, the hero sentence and the card buttons shrink toward illegible.

**Fix.** One display style, used once per view. Project titles become the largest text in each card. `.number` becomes a small label. Delete the redundant `.hero-intro`, or keep one sentence at a readable size. Replace opacity-dimmed body text with a solid muted color at weight 400. Set an 12px floor on card actions and thumbnail labels. Point homepage headings at the case-study scale.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable typeset`

### 4. [P1] Homepage copy is brochure language

**What.** Verbatim:

- Hero: "A PRODUCT DESIGNER CRAFTING INTUITIVE EXPERIENCES AT THE INTERSECTION OF UX & AI."
- About: "Hi there! I'm Saurabh, a product designer with over a decade of experience…" ending in "revitalize existing ones to better serve business needs."
- Product Design: "I bring the empathy that AI lacks and can make better design judgments." The same paragraph says "seamless" twice.
- Agentic engineering is introduced as a strikethrough meme: "~~Vibe Coding~~ Agentic engineering".
- Branding: "Since childhood, I've been fascinated by the design of Nike and Adidas logos."
- New age marketing: "high impact campaigns that drive revenue."
- Intent card: "Enhancing AI assistant's grasp of complex requests, including JEV and design."
- Jira card: "An AI-assisted Jira productivity tool built over a weekend." The study's own line is stronger, and the name disagrees with "JiraBro".
- Catalog card label: "Ease up the experience!"

**Why it matters.** A reader who has seen a hundred AI portfolios will discount the true claims, including the decade of practice and the shipped BMC work, because the sentences have no subject, no product, and no decision.

**Fix.** Put the case-study lines on the cards. Title the Jira project JiraBro everywhere. Rewrite the hero as one sourced sentence. Cut each Expertise item to one concrete sentence, or remove the section. Define JEV in the first sentence that uses it, or don't use the acronym on the card.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable clarify`

### 5. [P2] Case studies leak production notes, and the AI tells continue inside them

**What.** Intent still shows slots such as "Final conversational UI frame awaiting source asset." Diagram headers say "Editable SVG". JiraBro ends toward "Interactive sandbox — coming next." The detector's gradient-text and side-tab hits live mostly in these files, on top of an otherwise specific narrative. The token result and the decision story get surrounded by caveats written for the build, not for a reader.

**Why it matters.** The hedges are honest and they also read as unfinished. They sit on the pages that are currently the best argument for hiring.

**Fix.** Delete the empty asset slots and the "Editable SVG" labels. Keep one short "Evidence and scope" note per study. Leave the decision story and the labelled product UI.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable clarify`, then `/impeccable distill`
