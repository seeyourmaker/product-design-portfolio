# Redesigning Chat Experience Quick Look film

## Style brief

- **Subject and feeling:** The guest parking service request, moving from a long response to a clearer interaction. Cinematic, focused, confident.
- **Concept:** A journey inside one UI surface. Considered directions were a split-screen comparison and a cursor-led walkthrough; the continuous screen journey was chosen because it keeps the request and its result connected. Six visual beats: earlier table, lower continuation, approval choice, grouped draft, published card, service preview. Opens inside the old response and ends on the preview with “You can’t always chat.”
- **Palette and type:** Portfolio project blue `#87b5ea`, dark page `#0c0d10`, light text `#e9eaed`, all from the existing case study and homepage project thumbnail. Kanit is the portfolio display face; UI text remains inside the supplied images.
- **Text treatment:** One short editorial line at a time; the closing line replaces the final caption. No feature badges, subtitle track, or outcome figures.
- **Surface and motion:** Layered dark blue radial light behind one framed UI surface. The screen scrolls, wipes, and travels between supplied captures. A thin blue progress trace persists across the sequence. The approval choice gets the single bright outline moment.
- **Playback:** Silent 16:9 browser film, approximately 30 seconds, autoplaying and looping. A pause/play button sits beneath it; Space pauses when the film is focused and R restarts it. `?debug=1` adds a scrub slider for review. Reduced-motion preference shows a still final preview.

The animation uses the local product captures and final Figma exports listed in `redesign-chat-case-study-evidence.md`. The movement is editorial and does not represent recorded product interaction or a measured UX outcome.
