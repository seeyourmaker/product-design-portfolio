# OutlineButton

The secondary pill (`.live-button`) for opening a case study or Quick Look from a card.

- 2px `line-strong` border, no fill, `radius-pill`, `button-outline` label, 16px Lucide `ArrowUpRight` after it with `space-8` gap.
- Hover fills `wash-hover`. Sizes: `space-16` × `space-32` desktop; 12px text under 1024px; `space-12` × `space-16` and 10px text under 768px.
- Consumer supplies: label, icon, and onClick or href.
