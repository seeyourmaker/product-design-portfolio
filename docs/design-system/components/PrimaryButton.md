# PrimaryButton

The one gradient pill CTA (`.contact-button`); use it at most once per screen and only on dark grounds.

- Fill: `cta-grad-1` → `cta-grad-4` at 123deg (stops 7/37/72/100%), `shadow-cta`, 2px `white` outline inset 3px, `radius-pill`.
- Label: `button` style in `white`. Padding `space-16` × `space-48`; under 768px 12px text and `space-16` × `space-32`.
- Hover: lift 3px over `duration-fast`. Focus: global ring in `accent`.
- Consumer supplies: an action label ("Explore my work") and an href.
