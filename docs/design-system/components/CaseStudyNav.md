# CaseStudyNav

The single top row on every case study and Quick Look: a back link on the left, the project name in the accent on the right.

- `.cs-nav`: max `nav-max`, padding `space-32` `gutter` (`gutter-mobile` under 768px), 1px `line` underline, `case-nav` type.
- Back link: Lucide `ArrowLeft` 16px, `space-8` gap, turns `accent` on hover.
- Replaces `.jb-nav`, `.ic-nav`, `.adapt-nav`, `.chat-page-nav` and `.lg-case-nav` (paddings 22–32px, sizes 11–13px, tracking .05–.1em).
- Consumer supplies: back href (homepage `#projects` or history back) and the project name.
