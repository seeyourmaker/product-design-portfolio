# Redesigning Chat Experience: case study evidence

The owner supplied the earlier product captures in `docs/for redesign project` and the final Figma frames in [Concepts: Hybrid UI for assistants](https://www.figma.com/design/uWFDkuOYw2MFkxwfVF6W5U/-Concepts--Hybrid-UI-for-assistants?node-id=0-1&m=dev&focus-id=363-2088). The owner confirmed the redesign shipped, all designs may be shown publicly, and they led the work with two other designers. No measured outcomes or research findings were supplied.

The case study's before and after language describes visible interface differences. It does not claim that those differences produced a measured improvement. The Quick Look film remains a separate follow-up.

The Task diagram reuses `old-review.png` and `old-review-detail.png` as CSS-cropped excerpts. It calls out only details visible in those captures: expanded tables at the start, and cost, a fulfillment issue, and its action near the end. Both excerpts link to their unmodified full screenshots.
